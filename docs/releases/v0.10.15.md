# VibeMol 0.10.15

**Showing the first orbital keeps your view orientation**. After you rotate a molecule, choose an orbital in the Orbitals panel or the scene outliner. VibeMol centers the scene and adjusts the viewing distance to fit the surface while preserving the angle you chose. This works in both perspective and orthographic projection.

Reset view returns to the standard diagonal view at the distance fitted to the visible surface.
