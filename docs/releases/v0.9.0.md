# VibeMol v0.9.0

Open [VibeMol](https://vibemol.org/) or the opt-in [Workbench interface](https://vibemol.org/?workspaceLab=1). The default launch retains the original interface; Workbench study URLs keep experimental layout state separate and do not write normal autosave/recovery.

## Workspace and appearance

- A dockable Workbench with View, Measure, and Edit modes, a Panels menu, saved layouts, and a Focus view.
- A Properties inspector with Object and Look tabs, including mixed structure/orbital selections and per-object color and opacity overrides.
- Complete look tiles and independent Style and Color scheme selectors. Recipe references, per-axis modified indicators, resets, Undo, and portable user recipes keep saved and mixed appearances consistent.
- Shared atom, bond, and surface materials organized into Physical, Smooth (Phong), and Toon families. Basic uses Luminous; Gel and named preset materials remain available.
- Movable dialogs and inspectors, clearer camera coordinates, and coordinate copying and atom deletion from the Coordinates panel.

## Rendering and editing

- Improved shadows, including surfaces; adjustable surface opacity without molecule flicker.
- Continuous bond geometry, sphere-aware multiple-bond fitting, and consistent atom/bond materials and colors.
- Camera depth follows visible geometry with orbital padding, preventing clipping in large structures.
- Foreground-aware atom/bond picking and faster Edit interaction for large molecular systems.
- Hydrogen-only deletion preserves other coordinates and bypasses automatic hydrogen replacement and optimization.

## Files and preservation

- Complete portable session save/open, browser autosave, and explicit recovery.
- Molden orbitals are registered immediately and computed on demand; orbital selection shows the last selected orbital.
- New empty scenes and file imports, orbital-group appearance editing, and improved Psi4 frequency import.
- Headerless XYZ clipboard round-trips and a shared file/paste check offering bohr-to-angstrom conversion.
- Explicit surface palette polarity labels; the former Classic surface palette is now Tableau, with legacy files remaining readable.

Existing presets, named looks, and sessions remain readable. Old appearance snapshots migrate to separate Style and Color scheme references while retaining their rendered settings. Layout remains separate from scientific session state.
