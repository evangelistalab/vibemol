# VibeMol 0.10.17

The camera no longer moves when you show or hide orbitals or switch modes.
Use Reset view, Frame atoms or `view.set(fit=True)` to refit.

The Orbitals panel, the outliner and `orbitals.show` preserve position,
target, up and zoom when revealing a surface. Measure and Calculations
preserve the pose while changing surface visibility. New scenes, the first
atom in an empty scene, and Edit revealing a hidden target still frame the
content.

`view.setMode` resolves as soon as the requested mode applies. Its readback
still contains `mode` and `surfacesSuppressed`.
